# MuxMender 20261007-v56

## Choose how closely the output should match

New jobs offer Strict, Balanced (the default) and Space saver quality checks.
The app and automatic CLI use the same policy: mean VMAF 95, 94 or 90,
respectively, with fifth-percentile VMAF at least 90 for all three.
These scores are not percentages of retained picture quality.

The engine selects the smallest measured candidate that meets the chosen
checks. It never lowers a threshold after a failure. Resolution, savings,
timing, copied tracks, metadata and full-output validation still matter.
Existing queued jobs retain their recorded settings. A changed profile can
retest an automatic keep decision without undoing completed replacements.

## Fixes and usability

- Subtitle cues that outlast the pictures no longer cause false excessive
  sample-duration failures. Complete video packet timing proves the sample
  length; the original duration limit and decoded checks remain.
- NVIDIA bitrate presets use bounded VBR rather than CQ that discards the
  bitrate target and buffer. Ineffective CQ retries are removed from that
  bounded search. Original-resolution encoding is unchanged.
- Queue buttons explain why an action is unavailable, preserve safe pause
  availability during delayed polling, and resist stale responses.
- Individual waiting videos can be removed from the queue. The compact
  operations-terminal UI retains keyboard access and light/dark themes.
- Raw preset packet lists join terminal cleanup. Compact decisions, quality
  summaries, bitrate measurements, history and recovery receipts remain.
- Remote development observers distinguish stale progress from completion.

## What was tested

Real SDR excerpts with audio and subtitles were compared under all three
profiles on the RTX 5050. One case produced validated AV1 copies 40.97%
smaller under Strict/Balanced and a HEVC copy 56.15% smaller under Space saver.
Another produced 21.85% smaller copies. A third retained its original after
measured settings missed the excerpt savings target. Sources were unchanged.

All nine combinations of the three quality profiles and the 1080p, 720p and
480p presets passed generated-video GPU testing, including full-output bitrate
and technical validation. Lower-resolution visual scores use resized
references: they do not mean original-resolution detail is preserved.

These are bounded qualifications, not full-library, every-codec or universal
Plex playback certification. HDR-to-SDR, HDR resizing and real-media resizing
remain pending. Existing qualified original-resolution HDR preservation is
unchanged. Separate research utilities do not enable an unqualified route.

## Deployment

Build and smoke-test this exact archive on TrueNAS before installing it.
The stage script builds a separate image and tests generated media, the service,
API, cleanup, GPU encoding and the bundled reader. It never mounts your media
library or production output. Staging and testing do not resume the queue.
Keep the queue paused when installing. Start with a small Create copies batch.
